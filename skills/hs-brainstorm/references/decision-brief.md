# Decision Brief

Use this optional shape when a settled direction must survive the session or
feed `hs-plan`. Remove sections that do not apply.

```markdown
---
title: {short decision title}
date: {YYYY-MM-DD}
status: decided
---

# {Short decision title}

## Intent / Problem

## Desired Outcome

## Constraints

## Non-goals

## Domain Context

## Options Considered

| Option | Key assumption | Failure condition | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Decision

## Reasoning

## Acceptance Criteria

## Open Questions / Risks
```

The brief records why the direction was chosen. It is not a product overview,
implementation plan, or permission to perform external or destructive work.
